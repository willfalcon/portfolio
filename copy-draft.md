## Mississippi Department of Human Services

The state agency's public site: about 10 divisions, SNAP and child support applications, and more than 100,000 visitors a month.

**My part:** I led development and built the WordPress theme and the block library from scratch at Creative Distillery.

- **25+ custom Gutenberg blocks.** Editors build pages from blocks that only allow the design system's options, so new pages stay on brand without a developer.
- **County office lookup.** A Preact block that searches MDHS's Smartsheet data through a WordPress REST endpoint and saves a county's contacts as a PDF.
- **Procurement table from SharePoint.** Sortable, exportable listings pulled server-side from the agency's SharePoint lists.

Built for a state agency's accessibility requirements and checked against WCAG 2.2 AA.

Stack: WordPress, PHP, Preact, Sass, WP REST API, Smartsheet and SharePoint APIs

## Move to Learn TV

Five-minute movement videos for Mississippi classrooms, so teachers can find one fast and press play.

**My part:** I built the whole site, a Next.js frontend over the client's existing WordPress video library.

- **Streaming-style rows.** Category rows with hover cards that grow in place and stay inside the screen edge.
- **Search and favorites.** Debounced search against a custom WordPress endpoint; teachers save the videos their class repeats.
- **Links to every video.** `/video/…`, `/tag/…` and `/curriculum/…` URLs open the right player or list, so a teacher can share one link.

Stack: Next.js, React, styled-components, react-spring, headless WordPress, Sentry

## Skwahdle

A daily five-letter word game I built for my family. They passed it around for a while until it ran its course; I brought it back online as a work sample.

- **The day's word comes from Airtable,** so words can be scheduled in a spreadsheet without a deploy.
- **Guesses are checked server-side** against a 5,756-word list in a Netlify Function, so the answer list never reaches the browser.
- **Stats survived a rewrite.** Moving to Redux Toolkit changed the saved format, so a migration copies old streaks and guesses into the new store.

Stack: React, Redux Toolkit, styled-components, Netlify Functions, Airtable
